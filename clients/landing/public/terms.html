4. The Service

4.1 Free tier

The free tier costs nothing and needs no account, no email and no payment details. Its speed is limited (currently to 100 KB/s) so that it can stay free and is hard to abuse. Every country and every gateway is available on the free tier.

4.2 Premium

Premium raises the speed limit for one key, on every gateway at once, for the time you have paid for. If you restore the key on another device with its recovery key, the premium goes with it; it is meant for one device at a time, and devices using the same key on the same gateway disconnect each other. Premium is currently up to 200 Mbit/s for about a minute at a time and 50 Mbit/s under continuous use, with up to 256 open connections of each kind at once; there is no data cap. Actual speeds depend on the gateway you use, the network in between and your own connection, and are not guaranteed.

4.3 Changes to the Service

CumulusVPN is under active development. We may add, change or remove features, gateways, countries, connection methods and limits, including the free tier's speed limit, for example as the network grows or to deal with abuse.

4.4 What a VPN can and cannot do

CumulusVPN encrypts your traffic between your device and the gateway and lets you choose the country it leaves from. That protects you from snooping on your local network, from logging by your internet provider, and from some geo-restrictions. It is not anonymity software like Tor: websites can still recognize you through logins, cookies and browser fingerprinting, a gateway operator can see encrypted flow metadata in real time (see section 4.6 of our Privacy Policy), and no VPN can protect a device that is already compromised. Some websites and services block VPN traffic, and we do not guarantee access to any particular one. Traffic leaves the gateway for the wider internet just as it would leave your own connection, so use end-to-end encryption such as HTTPS for anything sensitive.
